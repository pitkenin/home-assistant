# Dashboards and menus

[Back to the project](../README.md)

Screenshots from the installed Home Assistant interface in October 2026. Some
menus are in Finnish. Account controls and reminder recipients are cropped out.

## Shopping

![Shopping catalogue organised into eight illustrated categories](../images/shopping-categories.webp)

The shopping catalogue has shortcuts for common products, grouped by category.
The counts show how many products are in each category, not how many need buying.

## Plants

![Six plant cards showing when their next checks are due](../images/plant-care.webp)

Part of the plant menu, showing when each plant is due for its next check.
“Huomenna” means tomorrow and “3 pv päästä” means in three days. The
[reminder notes](selected-builds.md#shared-reminders) cover scheduling and shared
completion.

## Lighting

![Lighting Studio showing room filters, automatic rules and a temporary manual hold](../images/lighting-studio.webp)

Lighting Studio shows the rules for each light, with room filters and access to
the button settings. The cards also show temporary manual overrides, such as the
bedroom light held until 06:00 in this screenshot.

## Music

![Music popup with separate Music and Assistant levels, room choices and genres](../images/music-controls.webp)

The music menu has separate Music and Assistant levels, room choices, genre
shortcuts and search. Music is at zero here while Assistant remains at 26%.
The [audio notes](selected-builds.md#separate-music-and-assistant-volume) explain
the firmware change behind those controls.
